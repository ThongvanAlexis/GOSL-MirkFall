# Phase 09.1 Plan 06: Clip owned by FogLayer, 4 builtin variants adapted Summary

**The four builtin renderers no longer compute or apply a clip — the host layer's single `clipPath(rect − discs)` is the only cut per frame and each variant paints the clipped identity frame; the reveal edge is rounded by a shared blurred `dstOut` stroke inside a `saveLayer`, `candlelight` centres its halo through `context.projectToScreen`, `heavenly_clouds`' CPU fallback carries a simplex tile anchored to world pixels (`pixelOrigin` / `zoomScale` through an `ImageShader` matrix, raw camera origin via `rawPixelOriginOf`), `solid_fill` is bit-identical whatever the camera fields carry, and the registry / factory / picker / tuner keep all four selectable and tunable — `MirkRenderer` still 3 members, `check_mirk_variant_file_count` still 6.**

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter. Additional notes:

- **Feather stroke width 4σ, centred on the edge.** A stroke of `[r − 2σ, r + 2σ]` blurred by σ gives ≈ 0.05 fog alpha at the cut and full fog at `r + 4σ` — softer than the old symmetric blur (0.5 at the edge) once the inner half is clipped away. Overlapping discs: each ring erases its own annulus; a ring portion inside another disc is clipped, a portion outside the union is within 2σ of the union boundary — the union edge is feathered, nothing else.
- **Fallback feathers keep their per-variant σ** (`featherRadiusFraction` × 4 px × pixelRatio for atmospheric / candlelight, 0.15 for heavenly, 0.1 for solid); only the mechanism changed.
- **Noise overlay blend.** `srcATop` rather than `srcOver`: keeps the body alpha (0.80 baseline stays 204 in the pre-shader test) and never paints where there is no fog; in the no-disc case (no `saveLayer`) that is the whole clipped frame, identical to `srcOver` over an opaque basemap.
- **Test geometry.** The default 100 m fixture disc is sub-pixel on the 1° test viewport (0.23 px); the new hole tests use `singleCentreDisc(radiusMeters: 8000)` (~18 px) so "centre transparent" is a real assertion.
